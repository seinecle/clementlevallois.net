A Course Taught by an AI Avatar

I'm exploring an idea: an online course delivered by an AI avatar of myself, where students can interrupt at any time to ask a question out loud. The lesson pauses, the avatar answers using the course material, and then picks up right where it left off.

These are research prototypes, not a product. I'm sharing them to exchange ideas with people who are innovating in education. I'd love to hear your thoughts: clementlevallois@gmail.com.

Three Iterations of the Prototype

  • 1️⃣ Nov 7, 2026. Video loop, lipsync. A marketing lesson delivered by the avatar, complete with slides. Students can ask questions by typing or speaking, and the avatar responds with citations from the course material it consulted. ✅ This prototype works 😐 The video is almost completely static. The voice is monotonous
  • 2️⃣ Nov 8, 2026. Multiple video loops, lypsinc. The next iteration, focused on improving the avatar's realism by creating multiple video loops for the different pedagogical phases. ✅ This prototype works 😟🤡 Multiple loops make the result actually worse. Transitions don't work and the idle poses are ridiculous.
  • Prototype 3: Real-time video generation. Work in progress, not yet online. In this version, each frame of the avatar is generated on the fly as it responds, rather than being drawn from a prerecorded video.

Good to Know

  • Answers are generated by a language model using only the course material. Questions outside the scope of the course are flagged as such.
  • The voice is a synthetic replica of my own, and the on-screen presenter is an avatar. This is made fully transparent to students.
