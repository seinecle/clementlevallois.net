A Course Taught by an AI Avatar

I'm exploring an idea: an online course delivered by an AI avatar of myself, where students can interrupt at any time to ask a question out loud. The lesson pauses, the avatar answers using the course material, and then picks up right where it left off.

These are research prototypes, not a product. I'm sharing them to exchange ideas with people who are innovating in education. I'd love to hear your thoughts: clementlevallois@protonmail.com.

Four Iterations of the Prototype

  • 1️⃣ Nov 7, 2026. Video loop, lipsync. A marketing lesson delivered by the avatar, complete with slides. Students can ask questions by typing or speaking, and the avatar responds with citations from the course material it consulted.
    ✅ This prototype works
    😐 The video is almost completely static, robotic. The voice is really monotonous. Not mobile friendly.

  • 2️⃣ Nov 8, 2026. Multiple video loops, lypsinc.
    The next iteration, focused on improving the avatar's realism by creating multiple video loops for the different pedagogical phases. ✅ This prototype works. It should be mobile friendly, too.
    😟🤡 Multiple loops make the result actually worse. Transitions don't work and the idle poses are ridiculous.

  • 3️⃣ Nov 9, 2026. Audio-driven generative video synthesis. I dropped the filmed video approach. The new approach relies on SoulX-FlashHead, a model released in 2026 that takes the image of a portrait as a starting point, and then animates it according to the incoming stream of audio input.
    ✅ This prototype works
    🎉 Audio and animation feel natural. Students can ask questions and the latency for the response is very short. In English or French.

  • 4️⃣ Nov 10, 2026. Lips animated in the student's browser. SoulX-FlashHead is kept for what can be prepared in advance: the narration and a short loop of the avatar's head. For live answers, a small model trained on these renders redraws the mouth directly in the student's browser, from the sound of the answer. No GPU server is needed when a student asks a question.
    ✅ This prototype works
    🎉 No GPU to start or to pay for when a question is asked: the avatar is animated from the very first answer, for any number of students at once.
    😐 Head movements come from a pre-rendered loop, so they don't follow what is being said, and the lips don't always close fully. Not tested on phones yet.
